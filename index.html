④ Eigen— · The Structure That Surfaces
What the compression reveals that the spreadsheet cannot

The dominant modes
were always there.

When you compress a tensor through a weight matrix, you do not just get scores — you get structure. Districts cluster. Patterns emerge that no analyst spotted in the spreadsheet. These are eigenmodes of the planning problem — the natural groupings the data itself insists on.

Td×i×t → Wαᵢ → vθ → Eig(Σd) → κnat
Eigenstructure · NPA Planning Insight
The map doesn't just show where. It shows why the clusters form.
Mode 1 — Structural poverty
Northern and northeastern districts load together on workforce gap, stunting, and facility access. Not one programme failure — a systemic deficit that runs across all indicators simultaneously.
Mode 2 — Urban complexity
Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono: high facility density but high mental health burden and youth unemployment. Resources present, outcomes degraded. A different intervention signature entirely.
Mode 3 — Revenue compliance
Western region districts cluster on CoC performance — they are Uganda's planning model. The eigenmode surfaces them as the replication target before any analyst writes a report.
Mode 4 — Climate exposure
Eastern flood-prone and northern drought-prone districts form a composite vulnerability mode that predicts health burden, agricultural failure, and fiscal stress simultaneously.
"The eigenstructure is Uganda showing you its skeleton. The map is just the skin."

⑤ Scalar · The Number They Act On
Everything collapses to one number — and that is the point

One answer.
Endlessly recomputable.

The scalar is what Ministers argue over at the budget table. The KPI. The headline. The certificate. But unlike the PDF's static number, this one recomputes every time a weight shifts, every time a district submits, every time the board asks a new question.

National Development Performance Index · Live
—
Composite · All Programmes · 146 Districts
Adjust the weights on slide ② — this number moves. That is what it means to own the scalar.
What it measures
A population-weighted composite of all active indicator scores across all 146 districts, under the board's current weighting. Not one ministry's KPI — Uganda's KPI.
Who controls it
The weights are NPA's. The data is NPA's. The keys are Government's. Ukubona maintains the engine. The scalar belongs to Uganda.
Why it matters for CoC
The Certificate of Budget Compliance checks alignment against static targets. The scalar makes the target dynamic — it reflects reprioritised weights in real time, not last year's assumptions.
The invariant
Swap the programme. The scalar recomputes. Swap the time period. The scalar recomputes. The chain never changes. Only the content changes. That is what makes it scalable to 2075.
Vision 2040 sets the scalar target. NDPIV sets the weights. The PIAP programmes are the vectors. The 146 districts are the tensor.
Ukubona is the compressor. Government owns the output. — The invariant · npa-04 · Ukubona LLC

The invariant · stated formally
The chain T → W → v → Eig → κ is the same for health, agriculture, revenue, climate, and governance. The only thing that changes is the slice of the tensor and the content of the weight matrix.
