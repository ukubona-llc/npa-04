① Tensor · The Full Picture
The raw data — before any question is asked

Uganda is a tensor.

146 districts. A hundred indicators. Measured across time. That is the tensor: a three-dimensional array — Districts × Indicators × Time — that holds the full complexity of Uganda without compressing or discarding anything. Every question the board ever asks is a slice of this tensor.

The key insight · scalability
The tensor does not care what the indicators are. Health today. Revenue compliance tomorrow. Climate vulnerability next year. Parish Development Model uptake in 2028. The architecture is invariant to the content. Add a new indicator: add a column. Add a new time period: add a layer. The chain does not change.

② Compressor · The Weight Matrix
The board's priorities encoded as mathematics

The weights are a political act.

The compressor is a matrix: each row is a district, each column is an indicator, each cell is a weight. Multiply the tensor by the weight matrix and you get a score per district. The score is the colour on the map. The weights are what the board believes matters — and that belief, previously informal, is now visible, auditable, and arguable.

④ Eigen— · The Structure That Surfaces
What the compression reveals that the spreadsheet cannot

The dominant modes
were always there.

When you compress a tensor through a weight matrix, you do not just get scores — you get structure. Districts cluster. Patterns emerge that no analyst spotted in the spreadsheet. Kampala and Arua both red, for different reasons. Karamoja blue on revenue compliance but red on health. These are eigenmodes of the planning problem — the natural groupings the data itself insists on.

Td×i×t → Wαᵢ → vθ → Eig(Σd) → κnat
Eigenstructure · NPA Planning Insight
The map doesn't just show where.
It shows why the clusters form.
Mode 1 — Structural poverty
Northern and northeastern districts load together on workforce gap, stunting, and facility access. Not one programme failure — a systemic deficit that runs across all indicators simultaneously. The twin names it. The PDF just lists the numbers.
Mode 2 — Urban complexity
Kampala, Wakiso, Mukono: high facility density but high mental health burden and youth unemployment. The eigenmode is urban compression — resources present, outcomes degraded. A different intervention signature entirely.
Mode 3 — Revenue compliance
Western region districts cluster on budget compliance (CoC) performance — they are Uganda's planning model. The eigenmode surfaces them as the replication target before any analyst writes a report.
Mode 4 — Climate exposure
Eastern flood-prone districts and northern drought-prone districts form a composite climate vulnerability mode that predicts health burden, agricultural failure, and fiscal stress simultaneously. The upstream signal the annual PDF never shows.
"The eigenstructure is Uganda showing you its skeleton. The map is just the skin."

⑤ Scalar · The Number They Fuss About
Everything collapses to one number — and that is the point

One answer.
Endlessly recomputable.

The scalar is what Ministers argue over at the budget table. The KPI. The headline. The number in the speech. The certificate. The scalar is the final compression — tensor through compressor through vector through eigenstructure, collapsed to a single national performance index. But unlike the PDF's static number, this one recomputes every time a weight shifts, every time a district submits, every time the board asks a new question.

National Development Performance Index · Live
—
Composite · All Programmes · 146 Districts
Adjust the weights on slide ② or change the vector on slide ③ and this number moves. That is what it means to own the scalar.
What it measures
A population-weighted composite of all active indicator scores across all 146 districts, under the board's current weighting. Not one ministry's KPI — Uganda's KPI.
Who controls it
The weights are NPA's. The data is NPA's. The keys are Government's. Ukubona maintains the engine. The scalar belongs to Uganda.
Why it matters for CoC
The Certificate of Budget Compliance currently checks alignment against static targets. The scalar makes the target dynamic — it reflects reprioritised weights in real time, not last year's assumptions.
The invariant
Swap the programme. The scalar recomputes. Swap the time period. The scalar recomputes. The chain never changes. Only the content changes. That is what makes it scalable to 2075.
